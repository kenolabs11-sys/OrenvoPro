# Orenvo Pro — Refund Policy

**Last updated: October 22, 2026**

This Refund Policy explains how refund requests are handled for Orenvo Pro, a digital desktop software product developed by KenoLabs.

Orenvo Pro is delivered as a digital license key. Because license access is delivered immediately after purchase and may be activated right away, refund eligibility may be subject to the conditions below and applicable law.

This policy does not limit any mandatory rights you may have under applicable law.

## 1. Digital Product Notice

Orenvo Pro is a digital software product.

After purchase, the customer receives access to a license key. The license key can be used to activate Pro features in the Orenvo Pro desktop app.

Once a license key has been delivered or activated, the product is considered digitally delivered. Delivery or activation alone does not automatically remove any statutory right of withdrawal where the applicable legal requirements for doing so have not been met.

## 2. Refund Review Window

As part of our voluntary refund-review process, refund requests may be reviewed within 3 days of purchase. This 3-day review window does not restrict, shorten, or replace any mandatory statutory withdrawal, cancellation, refund, or consumer-remedy period.

A refund may be considered if:

- the license key has not been activated,
- the purchase was accidental and the product has not been used,
- the customer purchased the same license more than once,
- there was a license delivery problem,
- there is an activation problem that KenoLabs cannot reasonably resolve,
- the transaction was fraudulent or unauthorized,
- a refund is required by applicable law,
- the payment provider requires or approves a refund under its own policies.

Submitting a voluntary refund request does not guarantee that a refund will be approved; statutory consumer rights remain unaffected.

## 3. Activated Licenses

Once a license key has been activated, the sale is normally final under our voluntary refund policy, except where applicable law provides otherwise.

Activated licenses are generally not eligible for discretionary refunds because Pro access has already been delivered and used. However, activation or digital delivery does not in itself waive mandatory statutory rights. Any loss of a statutory digital-content withdrawal right is subject to the requirements of applicable law, including, where required, the customer's prior express consent, acknowledgment, and order confirmation.

If you experience an activation or technical issue, contact support first. We will try to help resolve the issue before considering a refund.

## 4. Non-Refundable Situations

Subject always to rights that cannot be excluded under applicable law, refunds are generally not provided under our voluntary policy for:

- change of mind after activation,
- failure to read the product description or system requirements,
- preference for a different app after using the license,
- requests made after the voluntary review window,
- licenses that have been activated and used,
- license keys that have been shared, resold, leaked, or abused,
- attempts to bypass license, device, or usage limits,
- unsupported systems or workflows that were not advertised as supported,
- separate products, services, or future features not included in the purchased license.

## 5. Exceptions

KenoLabs may make exceptions at its discretion, especially for:

- duplicate purchases,
- mistaken purchases where the license was not activated,
- unresolved activation problems,
- clear technical delivery failures,
- fraudulent or unauthorized payments,
- legal requirements,
- payment provider decisions.

Each request is reviewed individually.

## 6. Discounted Orders

For discounted purchases, any approved refund is based on the amount actually paid by the customer, not the original list price, subject to applicable law and payment-provider requirements.

If a coupon or promotional discount reduces a purchase price to €0 (including a 100% discount), there is no customer payment to refund for that transaction. A legitimately issued promotional license may still be valid under the applicable license terms.

If a partial or full refund is processed, the payment provider and KenoLabs will consider the actual payment amount, the original transaction, and any statutory refund obligations. A refund will not exceed the amount actually received for that transaction unless applicable law requires otherwise.

## 7. Refunds, Chargebacks, and License Revocation

If an order is refunded, reversed, disputed, or charged back, the associated Orenvo Pro license may be revoked automatically.

After revocation:

- Pro access will stop,
- activated devices may be deactivated,
- the license key may no longer validate,
- the app may return to the free tier or limited mode.

Refunded, reversed, disputed, or charged-back orders do not continue to provide Pro access.

## 8. Chargebacks and Payment Disputes

If you have a problem with your purchase, contact support before opening a payment dispute or chargeback.

Chargebacks may cause immediate license revocation and may be handled by the payment provider according to its own rules.

Opening a dispute does not guarantee continued access to Orenvo Pro.

## 9. Processing Time

Approved refunds may take several business days to appear on your payment statement.

Processing time depends on the payment provider, bank, card network, and payment method.

## 10. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, contact: [contact@kenolabs.dev](mailto:contact@kenolabs.dev)

Include:

- your purchase email,
- order ID if available,
- license key short code if available,
- purchase date,
- reason for the request,
- screenshots or details if the issue is technical.

Do not send your full license key publicly or in public forums.

## 11. Payment Provider

New Orenvo Pro payments, receipts, refunds, and chargebacks are processed through Freemius, our current payment provider and Merchant of Record.

Transactions made previously through Lemon Squeezy remain associated with their original purchase records. Refunds and payment disputes for those transactions are handled through the applicable Lemon Squeezy transaction/payment process, in accordance with applicable law and the provider's rules.

The relevant payment provider may review refund requests, payment disputes, suspected fraud, and chargebacks according to its own terms and risk policies.

KenoLabs may not be able to override payment provider decisions. Mandatory statutory consumer rights remain unaffected.

## 12. Legal Rights

This Refund Policy is intended to explain KenoLabs’ standard refund process for Orenvo Pro.

Nothing in this policy limits rights that cannot be waived under applicable consumer law.

Where applicable law requires a different refund, cancellation, or withdrawal process, that law will apply.
